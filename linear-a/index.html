Linear A corpus: Minoan inscriptions and research resources

Linear A is a writing system used on Bronze Age Crete and elsewhere in the Aegean. Although some signs can be compared with those of Linear B, the language represented by most Linear A texts remains undeciphered. A transliteration is not a translation.

Explore the research corpus

The Linear A repository is the authoritative source for this project's current records, documentation, provenance, data formats, licensing, validation reports, and associated AI skill. This page is an entry point, not an independent scholarly edition.

What researchers can investigate

  • Attested inscriptions and their recorded identifiers, where supported by the repository's evidence.
  • Sign sequences, transliterations, and alternative readings, with uncertainty preserved.
  • Findspots, object contexts, source references, and links to accessible editions where available.
  • Documented gaps, provenance limitations, and unresolved questions.

What the corpus does not establish

Linear A has not been securely deciphered. Similar-looking signs do not by themselves prove equivalent phonetic values or word meanings. The availability of records or references does not imply permission to reproduce restricted source photographs, editions, or third-party databases. Consult the repository's rights documentation before reuse.

Sources and scholarly interpretation

For an introduction to the scholarly study of Linear A, consult established epigraphic editions and research literature, and verify every proposed reading against its cited evidence. This website does not claim independent expert certification of every record.

Contribute corrections

To suggest a correction, include the inscription identifier, proposed change, supporting publication or evidence, and uncertainty notes. Open a Linear A research issue or read the feedback guidelines.
