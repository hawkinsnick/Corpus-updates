This blog will bring project updates into one place. Each entry will explain what changed, link to the relevant repository or milestone, and make the remaining questions clear.

## What we are building

The project brings together ancient-script reference corpora, documentation, validation tools, and AI skills for researchers. The [Linear A repository](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-A) houses the master corpus tooling and combined AI skill.

Current work includes [Cretan hieroglyphs](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cretan-hieroglyphs) and [Cypro-Minoan](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Cypro-Minoan). Subsequent updates will link to specific published changes rather than treating unfinished branches as releases.

## What counts as evidence

Updates will distinguish catalogue records from readings, source assertions from independent observations, and software checks from scholarly review. A larger corpus is useful only when its provenance, uncertainty, and reuse conditions remain traceable.

## Source access and rights

Source access, reuse permission, and independent review remain separate issues. We do not have permission to redistribute GORILA material. A source being available online does not remove its reuse restrictions.

## What comes next

Publish evidence-backed milestone reports, with links to completed changes and the corresponding individual and master AI skills. Each report will include validation, unresolved source or scholarly questions, and the next concrete task.
