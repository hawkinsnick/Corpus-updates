# About the project

The ancient scripts corpus initiative develops reference datasets, documentation, and researcher-facing AI skills. This blog explains progress in plain language and links readers to the underlying evidence.

Each corpus has its own documentation and AI skill. The [master corpus hub](https://github.com/hawkinsnick/Linear-A) coordinates the combined skill and shared tooling. This blog is an editorial companion.

## How to read our updates

A successful software check establishes that the tested behavior works; it does not establish an inscription's reading, language, date, or interpretation. Source-attributed claims, provisional candidates, engineering validation, and independent scholarly review are recorded separately.

Public accessibility does not by itself establish permission to redistribute a source. Source-specific licenses and permissions govern reuse. We do not have permission to redistribute GORILA material; references to GORILA or secondary websites do not grant that permission.

The Egyptian hieroglyphic project and LightroomIsSlow are separate from the corpus initiative covered here.
